## Hospital ER Performance Dashboard (Excel)

### Business Problem
The objective of this project was to analyze emergency room (ER) performance to identify inefficiencies in patient flow, waiting times, and resource utilization, with the goal of improving operational efficiency and patient experience.

### Approach
- Cleaned and structured ER data for consistent reporting  
- Used Excel (Pivot Tables, advanced formulas) to aggregate patient and operational metrics  
- Built an interactive dashboard to track key KPIs such as patient volume, waiting time, and treatment efficiency  
- Designed monthly performance views to monitor trends over time  

### Key Insights
- Peak patient volumes occurred during specific time periods, leading to increased waiting times  
- Higher patient load was directly correlated with delays in treatment efficiency  
- Certain days/months consistently showed operational strain, indicating resource allocation gaps  
- Variations in patient flow highlighted opportunities to optimize staffing schedules  

### Impact
This analysis can help hospital management:
- Reduce patient waiting times through better resource planning  
- Optimize staff allocation based on peak demand periods  
- Improve overall ER efficiency and patient satisfaction  
- Support data-driven operational decisions in healthcare settings  

### Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas, Dashboard Design)  

### Features
- KPI tracking (Patient Volume, Waiting Time, Efficiency Metrics)  
- Monthly trend analysis  
- Interactive filtering for time-based insights
